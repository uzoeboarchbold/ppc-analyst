3. **Check the two "enabled" campaigns.** They're on but served nothing — that
   usually means bids are too low to win the auction, budget is effectively unused,
   or the targeted keywords have no search volume. Worth a look once you re-launch.
4. **Re-run this report after a couple of active days** to get real performance
   numbers (ACOS, ROAS, wasted spend, search-term winners) to optimise against.
