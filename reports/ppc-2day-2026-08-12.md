## Part B — Plain-English Summary

**The headline: the ads were switched on but essentially didn't run.** Across the two days
your Sponsored Products campaigns were shown about **120 times** but got **zero clicks**,
so you spent **$0.00** and — unsurprisingly — made **$0.00** in ad-attributed sales.
With no spend and no sales there is no ACOS or ROAS to report for this window.

**What this means in practice:** 120 impressions over two full days is extremely low, and
0 clicks from 120 impressions means shoppers who did see the ads didn't click. Two likely
causes, in order of probability:
1. **Bids are too low to win real placements.** Your top-of-search impression share is
   ~1% at best and 0% on most campaigns — that means your ads almost never appear at the
   top of search, where the clicks are. Low bids keep you in cheap, low-visibility slots.
2. **Genuinely tiny volume / narrow targeting.** The keyword-root campaigns (Litter+Urine,
   Odor Root) delivered 0–2 impressions, suggesting the targets are very niche or bids are
   far below market.

**Best "performer":** None truly performed, but the *Isolated — Pet Odor Eliminator (Phrase)*
campaign had the only non-trivial top-of-search share (1.04%) — it's the closest to being
visible. The *Cat Tunnel Bed (Exact)* and *Home+Eliminator+Brand (Exact)* campaigns pulled
the most impressions (41 and 37).

**Worst:** *Litter+Urine Root* and *PT | ST w/ Sales* got **0 impressions** — they're not
serving at all. Worth checking their bids, budgets, and whether their targets are still valid.

**Wasted spend / negatives to add:** None this window — there was no spend to waste, and no
search-term data was returned (Amazon only reports search terms once ads earn billable
clicks). Nothing to add as a negative keyword right now.

**Well-converting search terms to add as exact-match:** None available — with 0 clicks there
were no converting search terms to harvest this window.

**What to do next (priority order):**
1. **Raise bids** on the enabled campaigns, especially the top-of-search-focused ones, so the
   ads actually win clickable placements. A top-of-search IS near 0% is the core problem.
2. **Check the two 0-impression campaigns** (Litter+Urine Root, PT | ST w/ Sales) — confirm
   budgets aren't $0/exhausted, bids aren't far below market, and targets are still valid.
3. **Confirm the products are in stock and buy-box eligible** — ads can be throttled when a
   listing loses the buy box or goes low on inventory.
4. **Re-check in the next 2-day report** to see whether the bid changes lifted impressions,
   clicks, and top-of-search share.
