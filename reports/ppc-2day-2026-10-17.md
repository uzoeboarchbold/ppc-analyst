- **Spend:** $0.00
- **Sales:** $0.00
- **ACOS / ROAS:** Not applicable — you can't have an ad cost ratio with no ad spend.
- **Best campaign:** None — all enabled campaigns delivered nothing.
- **Worst campaign / wasted spend:** None — nothing spent means nothing wasted. There are no negatives to add and no winning search terms to promote, because no searches reached your ads.

**Why this matters:** The machinery is set up (54 campaigns built, 15 switched on) but the engine isn't turning over. For two days — and, on the wider check, for the past month — your Sponsored Products ads reached nobody.

### What to do next
1. **Check product eligibility first.** Confirm the advertised ASIN(s) are in stock, buyable, and hold the Buy Box. An ineligible product is the single most common reason enabled ads show zero impressions. This is free to check and fixes the problem instantly if it's the cause.
2. **Raise bids and daily budgets on 2–3 priority campaigns.** $1.50–$2.50/day with auto-bidding is likely too low to win any placement. Pick your best products, lift daily budgets to a level you're comfortable testing (e.g. $10–$15), and raise bids so the campaigns can actually enter auctions.
3. **Start with the Auto campaign.** "SP Auto" is the quickest way to see whether the listing can attract *any* traffic. If even a well-funded auto campaign gets no impressions, the problem is listing eligibility, not bids.
4. **Re-check after 48 hours.** Once ads are serving, the next 2-day report will have real numbers to optimise.
