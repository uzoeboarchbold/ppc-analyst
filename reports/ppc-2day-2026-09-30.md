### Why nothing is serving (most likely causes, in order)
1. **Bids too low to win auctions.** With so few impressions across many
   enabled campaigns, your keyword bids are probably below what it takes to show
   at all. This is the most common cause of "enabled but silent" campaigns.
2. **Very low daily budgets ($1.50).** Even if bids were competitive, $1.50/day
   leaves almost no room to compete.
3. **Listing availability.** If the advertised products (e.g. B0FXW3GW5F and the
   cat-bed ASINs) are out of stock, suppressed, or lost the Buy Box, Amazon
   stops serving their ads regardless of bid or budget. Worth checking directly
   in Seller Central.

### What to do next (this week)
1. **Confirm the products are live and in stock** with the Buy Box in Seller
   Central — if not, fix that first; nothing else matters until then.
2. **Raise bids** on a few priority campaigns to at least the suggested-bid
   range Amazon shows, so they can actually enter auctions.
3. **Lift daily budgets** on those same 2–3 priority campaigns from $1.50 to a
   level that allows meaningful testing (e.g. $10–15/day).
4. **Re-check in 2–3 days.** Once impressions and clicks start flowing, this
   report will begin producing real ACOS/ROAS numbers and negative-keyword /
   exact-match suggestions.
