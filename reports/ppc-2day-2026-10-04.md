## Part B — Summary (plain English)

- **Overall spend:** $0.00. **Sales:** $0.00. **ACOS/ROAS:** not applicable
  (nothing was spent and nothing sold through ads).
- **Best / worst campaign:** none — every campaign performed identically at
  zero, so there is nothing to rank.
- **Wasted spend / negatives to add:** none to flag, because no money was
  spent. The problem is the opposite of wasted spend: the budget isn't being
  used at all.
- **Well-converting search terms to add as exact-match:** none available — with
  no clicks or impressions there are no search terms to harvest.
- **The real issue — ads aren't being shown.** 15 campaigns are enabled with a
  combined $23.50/day budget, but they received **zero impressions**. When
  *every* campaign gets zero impressions for weeks, it is almost never a bidding
  tweak — it's usually one of:
  1. **The product listing can't be advertised** — out of stock, lost the Buy
     Box, or the listing was suppressed/deactivated. (Most likely given the
     sudden, total stop on 2 Sep.)
  2. **Bids set far below the market rate**, so the ads never win a placement.
     The very small $1.50/day budgets and low bids point this way too.
  3. A billing/payment hold on the ad account pausing delivery.

### What to do next (in order)
1. **Check the product listing(s) first** — confirm ASIN **B0FXW3GW5F** (and the
   cat tunnel bed) are **in stock, active, and holding the Buy Box**. This is the
   single most likely cause of zero impressions. No amount of PPC tuning works
   if the listing can't show ads.
2. **Check the ad account for a billing/payment hold** in Seller Central →
   Campaign Manager.
3. **Once the listing is healthy**, raise bids meaningfully (current bids appear
   too low to win auctions) and lift the tiny $1.50/day budgets so the ads can
   actually compete.
4. Re-check in the next report. If impressions return, we can then start
   optimising (negatives, exact-match harvesting, ACOS) — but none of that is
   possible until the ads are delivering again.
