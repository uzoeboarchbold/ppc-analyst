## Part B — Summary (plain English)

**The account was almost completely quiet over these two days.** Across all 9 enabled campaigns
there were **119 ad impressions but only 1 click**, which turned into **1 sale**.

- **Total spend:** $2.65
- **Total sales:** $13.99
- **ACOS (ad cost ÷ sales):** 18.9% — for every $1 of sales we spent about 19¢ on ads. That is healthy (lower is better; under ~30% is generally good).
- **ROAS (sales ÷ ad cost):** 5.28 — we earned $5.28 in sales for every $1 spent. Also healthy.

**Best campaign:** *SP Isolated … Pet Odor Eliminator Phrase* — the only campaign that spent
anything and the only one that sold. 1 click, $2.65 spend, 1 order, $13.99 sales (ROAS 5.28).

**Worst / underperforming:** Nothing "lost money," because only one campaign spent at all. The real
problem is **near-zero traffic**: several campaigns showed impressions but got **no clicks** —
*Cat Tunnel Bed Pink Only* (46 impressions, 0 clicks), *Home + Eliminator Root – Exact*
(34 impressions, 0 clicks), and *PT Pet Odor Eliminator* (15 impressions, 0 clicks). They are
being seen but not clicked, which usually points to bids too low to reach good placements, or
listing images/price/title not compelling enough at the placements we do get.

**Wasted spend / negatives to add:** None this period — there was no spend without sales, so there
are no obvious money-wasting search terms to add as negatives right now.

**Well-converting search terms to add as exact-match:**
- **"pet odor eliminator"** — currently running as *phrase* match and it produced the account's only
  sale (1 order from 1 click, ACOS 18.9%). Recommend adding it as an **exact-match** keyword (in the
  brand/exact campaign) so we can bid it more precisely and protect the term. It is the one proven converter.

**What to do next (priority order):**
1. **Add "pet odor eliminator" as exact match** and give it a dedicated, slightly higher bid — it is
   the only term converting and deserves stronger placement.
2. **Raise bids modestly on the high-impression / zero-click campaigns** (Cat Tunnel Bed, Home +
   Eliminator Exact, PT Pet Odor Eliminator). They get impressions but no clicks — a bid increase to
   reach top-of-search, plus a quick check of the main image/price, should start generating clicks.
3. **Check total budget/traffic:** 119 impressions in two days is very low for 9 campaigns. Confirm
   campaigns are not budget- or bid-capped and that the products are in stock and buyable.
4. Keep an eye on the winner's ACOS — at 18.9% there is room to scale spend on "pet odor eliminator"
   before efficiency suffers.
