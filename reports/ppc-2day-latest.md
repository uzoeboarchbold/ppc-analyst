- **Wasted spend:** None, because nothing was spent. There is nothing to add as negative keywords.
- **Well-converting search terms to add as exact-match:** None to suggest — with no clicks there
  is no search-term data this period.
- **The real issue:** Two enabled campaigns are getting **no impressions at all**. When a live
  campaign shows zero impressions for weeks, the usual causes are:
  1. **Bids too low** to win any ad auctions (most common).
  2. **Campaign/portfolio budget not actually delivering**, or a start/end date in the past.
  3. **The advertised product isn't buyable** — out of stock, lost Buy Box, or not in the
     targeted marketplace — so Amazon won't serve the ads.
  4. **Targeting is empty or paused** underneath an enabled campaign (e.g. all keywords paused,
     or auto-targeting groups switched off).

### What to do next
1. **Check the product first:** is the advertised ASIN in stock and holding the Buy Box on
   Amazon US? If not, fix that — ads won't run without it, and no bid change will help.
2. **Check bids:** raise keyword/target bids toward Amazon's suggested range; a $40/day budget
   with $0 spend almost always means bids are below the auction floor.
3. **Confirm the campaigns have active targets** (keywords/products) that are themselves ENABLED,
   and that the campaign start date isn't in the future and end date isn't in the past.
4. Once impressions start flowing, this report will begin showing real spend/sales and the
   optimisation advice (negatives, exact-match winners) becomes meaningful.
