there simply wasn't enough activity to judge efficiency.

- **Sales:** $0.00 — zero orders.
- **ACOS / ROAS:** not applicable (no sales).
- **Best campaign:** hard to crown one on this little data, but *"SP · Cat
  Tunnel Bed Pink Only · Exact Ranking"* is the only campaign that actually got
  clicks (3 clicks, 3.80% CTR) — so it's the only thing generating engagement.
- **Worst campaign:** none is "burning money" (total spend is under a dollar),
  but the *"Pet Odor Eliminator (Phrase)"* campaign is worth watching: it pulled
  46 impressions with a healthy 25% top-of-search share yet got **0 clicks** —
  it's showing up but nobody is clicking.
- **Wasted spend:** essentially none this window. The only campaign that spent
  ($0.75) did so on a term still in a "ranking" push, so I would **not** flag it
  as waste yet. No negative-keyword additions are warranted on 3 clicks and zero
  spend elsewhere.
- **Search terms to add as exact-match:** none qualify yet. A term should
  convert (or at least click well) before promoting it to exact — and nothing
  converted. The candidates to *keep an eye on* are "cat tunnel bed" (the only
  clicking term) and "pet odor eliminator" (lots of impressions, high placement,
  but no clicks — likely a listing/image or price issue rather than a keyword
  issue).

**What to do next**
1. **The real issue is volume, not efficiency.** 180 impressions and 3 clicks
   across 15 campaigns in 2 days means bids and/or budgets are throttling almost
   everything. If the goal is growth, raise bids on the enabled exact/phrase
   campaigns so they actually enter the auction.
2. **Investigate "Pet Odor Eliminator (Phrase)":** good visibility (25% top-of-
   search) but a 0% click rate points to the product listing (main image, title,
   price, rating) rather than the keyword. Worth a listing review.
3. **Give "Cat Tunnel Bed" room to prove out.** It's the only engagement you
   have — let it accumulate clicks before judging, and check the search-term
   report again next window for its first conversions.
4. **Nothing to cut.** Spend is too low to justify pausing or adding negatives
   this cycle.
