  the opposite problem: budget sitting idle and sales you're not making.)
- **Search terms to add as exact-match:** None available — with no clicks,
  there are no converting search terms to harvest this period.

**Why this is happening:** Your US Amazon Ads profile has **no valid
payment method**. Amazon pauses all delivery when billing can't be charged,
even while campaigns show as "enabled." I confirmed this isn't a one-day
fluke — looking back over 8–18 Nov, every campaign shows zero impressions
every single day. So the ads have been dark for at least two weeks.

**What to do next (in order):**
1. **Fix billing today.** Add/update a valid card in Seller Central
   (Account Info → Billing) and Amazon Ads (Billing & payments). This is
   the single thing unblocking everything else.
2. **Confirm delivery resumes.** Within 24–48h of fixing billing, check
   that impressions start appearing. If they don't, verify the account is
   in good standing and the product listings are active and in stock.
3. **Then optimise.** Once ads are serving again, the next reports will
   finally have real data to work with — at that point we can look at
   wasted spend, negatives, and winning search terms to add as exact-match.
   Until billing is fixed, no optimisation can make any difference.
