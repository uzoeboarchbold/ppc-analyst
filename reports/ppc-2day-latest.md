## ⚠️ Read this first — your ads are not running

The data pulled cleanly from Amazon, and it shows **no activity at all**: zero
impressions, zero clicks, zero spend and zero sales from Sponsored Products on
both days. This is **not** a reporting error — the Amazon Ads API returned your
live campaign list successfully, it simply reported zero metrics against every
campaign. To be sure, I also checked the **previous two weeks (21 Oct – 4 Nov)**
and it is the same story: **zero across the board**.

**Most likely cause:** the US seller account currently has **no valid payment
method on file** (Amazon's own flag `validPaymentMethod = false` on this
account). When Amazon can't charge for ad clicks, it stops delivering your ads
— so your 14 enabled campaigns are switched on but invisible to shoppers.

**What this means in plain terms:** you are not paying anything for ads right
now, but you are also getting **no ad-driven visibility or sales**. For a
product that relies on Sponsored Products to rank, every day dark is lost
traffic and lost organic rank momentum.

**Fix (takes a few minutes, needs you):** In Seller Central go to
**Settings → Account Info → Payment Information → Advertising / Charge Method**
and add or update a valid card. Ad delivery normally resumes within a few hours
of a valid payment method being accepted. (A separate possibility is a wider
account/billing hold — if the card is fine, check Account Health and any
billing notifications.)

Nothing below needs changing in the campaigns themselves yet — until billing is
restored there is no performance to optimise.
