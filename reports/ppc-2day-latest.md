## Part B — Summary (plain English)

**The account was almost completely quiet over these two days.**

- **Spend:** $0.08 total.
- **Sales:** $0.00 — no orders came from ads.
- **Clicks:** just 1 click, from 287 ad views (impressions).
- **ACOS / ROAS:** not meaningful with zero sales.

**Best campaign:** None stood out — the only campaign that got a click was *"Cat Tunnel Bed Pink Only – Exact Ranking – Louise"* (1 click, 2.5% click-through, 8 cents spent). It at least attracted interest but did not convert.

**Worst / wasted spend:** There is effectively no wasted spend to worry about, because barely anything was spent. Two campaigns showed people your ads but got no clicks — *"Pet Odor Eliminator Phrase"* (131 views) and *"Home + Eliminator + Brand Root Exact"* (82 views). That's normal at this tiny volume and not yet a reason to add negatives.

**Search terms to add as exact-match:** None qualify. Only one search term recorded any activity — *"cat tunnel bed"* (12 views, 1 click, no sale) — which is not enough evidence to promote to an exact-match keyword yet.

**What to do next:**
1. **Check why volume is so low.** 287 impressions across the whole account in 2 days is unusually small. Likely causes: bids set below the market rate, very low daily budgets, or campaigns recently (re-)enabled and still ramping. Worth confirming bids and budgets are actually live and competitive.
2. **Nudge the one campaign with traction** ("Cat Tunnel Bed Pink … Louise") — a small bid increase could turn its clicks into a sale and give a first data point on conversion.
3. **Leave negatives alone for now** — there isn't enough spend or click data to justify cutting any search terms.
4. **Re-measure in the next report.** With this little data, a single sale would swing every metric, so trends only become meaningful once daily impressions climb into the thousands.
