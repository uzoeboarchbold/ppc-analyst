## Part B — Plain-English summary

**The short version:** Over these two days the ads were shown **99 times but got zero clicks, spent $0.00, and made no sales.** Nothing was wasted (there was no spend), but nothing was earned either — the ads simply aren't pulling clicks.

- **Spend:** $0.00 · **Sales:** $0.00 · **ACOS:** n/a · **ROAS:** n/a (no spend or sales to measure).
- **Impressions:** 99 across 8 campaigns — so the ads *are* appearing in front of shoppers, just at a very low volume (~50/day) and almost never at the top of search.
- **Best campaign:** None stood out — none produced a click or a sale. The only faint bright spot is *"SP Isolated … Pet Odor Eliminator Phrase,"* which had the highest top-of-search share (17%), meaning it at least reached prominent placements.
- **Worst campaign:** Nothing is actively losing money (spend is $0), but *"SP Home + Eliminator + Brand Root … Exact"* got the most impressions (27) with **0 clicks**, which is the clearest sign that shoppers are seeing the ad and scrolling past it.
- **Wasted spend / negatives to add:** None to suggest — there was no spend, so nothing to trim and no negative keywords needed this window.
- **Search terms to add as exact-match:** None available — with zero clicks, Amazon returned no search-term data for this window, so there are no proven converting terms to promote yet.

**Why this is happening:** The account is in a low-volume / ramp-up state. Ads win a trickle of impressions but sit low on the page (top-of-search share is mostly 0%), and bids appear too low to win clicks. For context, the account has had **only 1 order in the last ~2 weeks** (a $13.99 sale on 7 Aug); most days record 0 clicks.

### What to do next
1. **Raise bids** on the enabled campaigns (especially the exact-match ones getting impressions but 0 clicks). Top-of-search share near 0% means the ads rarely reach the spots that actually get clicked.
2. **Add a top-of-search placement bid adjustment** (e.g. +25–50%) on the best-structured campaigns to buy more prominent placements while volume is tiny and cheap.
3. **Check the listings behind the ads** — 99 impressions with 0 clicks can also mean the main image, price, or title isn't compelling. Worth a quick look at the click-through fundamentals.
4. **Don't add negatives yet** — there's no wasted spend to cut. The priority is getting clicks flowing, not trimming.
5. **Re-check next run** whether higher bids turn impressions into clicks; that's the single metric to watch.
