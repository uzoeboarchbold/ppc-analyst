## Part B — Summary (plain English)

**The account was almost completely quiet over these two days.**

- **Spend:** $0.25 total over 2 days.
- **Sales:** $0.00 — no orders came from ads.
- **ACOS:** not calculable (you can't have an advertising cost of sale with no
  sales). **ROAS:** 0.
- **Traffic:** ads were shown 202 times and got just 1 click. Only 6 of your 54
  campaigns showed at all; the other 48 got zero impressions.

**Best campaign:** none really performed — but the only click came from
*"Cat Tunnel Bed Pink Only"* (76 impressions, 1 click, 25¢). It at least earned
traffic.

**Worst / wasted spend:** there is essentially no wasted spend to cut, because
almost nothing spent. The single 25¢ click on "cat tunnel bed" did not convert,
but one click is far too little data to judge or add as a negative.

**Well-converting search terms to add as exact-match:** none — there were no
conversions in the window, so there is nothing to promote to exact yet.

**The real story is under-delivery, not efficiency.** 202 impressions across
two days for a 54-campaign account is very low, and top-of-search impression
share is only ~2–5% where it registers at all. That pattern usually means one
of: bids set too low to win auctions, daily budgets exhausted early or set very
low (account daily budget shows as $40), or campaigns recently paused/limited.

**What to do next:**
1. **Check delivery status first.** Confirm campaigns aren't out of budget or
   sitting far below the auction-winning bid — the near-zero impressions are the
   priority, not ACOS.
2. **Raise bids on the isolated Phrase campaigns** that got impressions but no
   clicks (Pet Odor Eliminator, Cat Deterrent Spray) so they can move higher on
   the page and actually earn clicks.
3. **Give it a longer look-back.** Two days with 1 click is too thin to act on
   for keyword/negative decisions — revisit with the 7-day report before
   pruning or scaling anything.
4. **No negatives or exact-match additions this cycle** — insufficient data.
