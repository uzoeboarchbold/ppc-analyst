## Part B — Summary (plain English)

- **Overall:** In the 2 days of 30–31 August, the ads were shown **331 times**,
  got **1 click**, cost **$0.25**, and produced **no orders and $0 in sales**.
- **What this means:** The account is essentially idle right now. Spend is
  tiny, so there is no "wasted spend" to worry about — but there's also almost
  no activity to learn from. Impressions are trickling in, but ads are almost
  never appearing at the top of search (top-of-search impression share is
  around 0.1%), which is why clicks are so scarce.
- **Best campaign:** None stood out — the only click came from the
  *Cat Tunnel Bed* campaign, but it didn't convert.
- **Worst campaign:** Nothing is burning money. The concern is the reverse:
  several enabled campaigns (Pet Odor Eliminator, Brand Root, Auto) are getting
  impressions but effectively **zero visibility at the top of search**, so they
  can't earn clicks.
- **Wasted spend / negatives to add:** None this window — spend was only $0.25
  and no search term wasted money.
- **Well-converting search terms to add as exact-match:** None yet — there
  were no sales in this window, so there's nothing proven to promote.
- **Likely root cause:** Bids and/or budgets are too low to win placements.
  With 331 impressions across 2 days and ~0.1% top-of-search share, the ads
  are being out-bid for the placements that actually get clicked.

### What to do next
1. **Raise bids** on the enabled research campaigns (Pet Odor Eliminator,
   Brand Root, Auto) so they can win a share of top-of-search placement —
   that's where clicks come from.
2. **Check daily budgets aren't capping too early.** The US profile daily
   budget shows $40; confirm campaigns aren't set far below what's needed.
3. **Give it a few days after a bid increase**, then re-check. There isn't
   enough data yet to cut or scale anything responsibly.
4. **Fix the two automation issues** (enable Gmail in this chat; correct the
   `AMZ_PROFILE_ID` to the US numeric id `26765323558215`) so future runs are
   fully hands-off.
