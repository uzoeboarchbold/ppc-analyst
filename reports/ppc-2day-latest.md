### What this means and what to do next

Zero **impressions** (not merely zero clicks) almost always means one of these,
in order of likelihood:

1. **The product can't be advertised right now.** The most common cause is the
   listing being **out of stock, suppressed, or not holding the Buy Box** for
   ASIN B0FXW3GW5F. Amazon will not show ads for a product shoppers can't buy.
   → *Check the listing in Seller Central: is it active, in stock, and winning
   the Buy Box?* This is the first thing to verify.
2. **Bids are far below the market.** Budgets are tiny ($1.50/day on most
   campaigns) and if the bids are also very low, you may never win an auction.
   → *If the listing is healthy, raise bids on 2–3 core campaigns and watch for
   impressions over the next 48h.*
3. **A portfolio or account-level cap / payment issue** is pausing delivery.
   → *Check for an account-level budget cap, a billing/payment failure, or a
   portfolio end-date in Seller Central.*

**Recommended next action:** confirm the ASIN is buyable (in stock + Buy Box)
first, since that gates everything else. If it is, nudge bids up on the main
"Odor Root", "Cat Deterrent Exact" and "Auto" campaigns and re-check in two
days. I'll keep flagging this on every run until impressions start appearing.
