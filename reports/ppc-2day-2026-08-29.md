- **Wasted spend:** essentially none to flag. $0.75 total spend is far too
  small to judge, and no single search term has burned money without
  converting. **No negative keywords are recommended yet** — there isn't
  enough data.
- **Well-converting search terms to add as exact-match:** **none yet** — no
  search term has produced a sale, so there is nothing proven to graduate to
  exact-match. (Note: "cat tunnel bed" is already running as a keyword.)

**What to do next**
1. **Let it run and gather data.** Two days with 3 clicks is far too little
   to make optimisation decisions. Give it 7–14 days.
2. **Check why 8 campaigns show zero impressions** — usually bids too low
   or budgets/keywords too narrow. If these products should be selling,
   nudge bids up so they start showing.
3. **Confirm budgets are intended.** The account's daily budget cap is $40
   but only $0.75 was spent over two days — spend is being throttled by low
   bids or limited keyword reach, not by budget.
4. **Fix the two setup items** in the notes above (profile ID + Gmail) so
   future runs are fully automatic and cover the right marketplace(s).
