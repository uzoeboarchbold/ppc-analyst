### Why was there no activity?
Two campaigns are switched ON ("SP KT | ST w/ Sales" and "SP PT | ST w/ Sales",
each with an $8/day budget, both created 2026-06-09), but they showed **zero
impressions** on Saturday and Sunday. Common reasons:

1. **Bids too low** — if your keyword/target bids are below the going rate,
   Amazon simply won't show your ads.
2. **Brand-new campaigns still warming up** — they were only created on 9 June
   and may have very few keywords/targets, or weak targets.
3. **Everything else is paused** — the rest of the account (the cat-deterrent /
   pet-odour and cat-tunnel-bed campaigns) is off, so there's no other source of
   ad impressions.

### What to do next
1. **Check the two enabled campaigns in Seller Central** — confirm they contain
   keywords/targets and that those targets are not paused.
2. **Raise the bids** on those campaigns to a level that can actually win
   placements (start by matching Amazon's suggested bid), so they begin
   collecting impressions and clicks.
3. **Decide whether to re-enable** some of the previously-active campaigns (e.g.
   the pet-odour / cat-deterrent set) if you intend to keep selling those ASINs.
4. If the account is **intentionally paused**, no action is needed — but expect
   $0 ad sales until something is switched back on.
