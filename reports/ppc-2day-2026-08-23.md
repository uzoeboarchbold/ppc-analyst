1. **Bids too low** — daily budgets are tiny ($1.50–$2.50 on the active campaigns), and bids may be below what it costs to win a spot, so ads rarely show.

3. **New/low-history campaigns** still ramping.

3. **Review the 14 zero-impression campaigns** — decide which to activate (raise bids) and which to leave paused; right now they contribute nothing.
