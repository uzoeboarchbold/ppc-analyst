## Part B — Summary (plain English)

**The short version:** Over 10–11 Sep your Amazon ads spent **$0** and made
**$0** in sales, because they simply were not shown to anyone — zero
impressions across all 15 live campaigns. So the usual health numbers
(ACOS, ROAS, CTR) can't be calculated; there's no activity to measure.

**Is this a mistake in the report?** No. We double-checked by pulling the
day-by-day history: your ads had a tiny trickle of activity on 31 Aug and
1 Sep (a couple of hundred views, 1 click a day, a few cents spent, no
sales), and then went to **zero from 2 Sep onwards**. So the "off switch"
appears to have flipped around 2 Sep and nothing has served since.

**Best / worst campaigns:** Not applicable this period — with zero data
every campaign is tied at nothing. There is no wasted spend to cut and no
winning search term to promote, simply because no auctions were entered.

**Why are live campaigns showing nothing?** The 15 enabled campaigns each
have a very small **$1.50/day budget**. Ads that serve nothing at all
(not even a single impression over two weeks) usually point to one of:
1. **Bids set too low** to win any auction at current market prices — the
   most common cause when budgets are this small.
2. **The product isn't buyable** — out of stock, lost Buy Box, or a
   suppressed/inactive listing means Amazon won't show the ad.
3. Campaigns were **deliberately turned down/paused** around 2 Sep and the
   live ones aren't competitive on their own.

## What to do next (priority order)

1. **Confirm the products are live and in stock** with the Buy Box. If a
   listing is out of stock or has lost the Buy Box, ads stop instantly —
   fix this first, it's the most likely single cause.
2. **Check the bids on the 15 enabled campaigns** against Amazon's
   suggested bid. If bids are below the suggested range, raise them toward
   it so the ads can actually win placements.
3. **Revisit the $1.50/day budgets.** They're not the blocker while spend
   is $0, but once bids/stock are fixed they will cap delivery quickly —
   plan to raise the budgets on your proven campaigns.
4. **Re-run this report in 2 days.** Once ads are serving again we'll have
   real numbers to optimise (negatives for wasted spend, exact-match adds
   for converting terms). None of that is possible until impressions return.
