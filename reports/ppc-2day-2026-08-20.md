  barely appearing (a handful of impressions, mostly single digits per
  campaign) and winning **no clicks at all**. This is almost always caused by

  never rank high enough for a shopper to click. Top-of-search impression share
  is in the low single digits (best was 13% on "pet odor eliminator"),
  confirming the ads rarely reach the top of page 1 where clicks happen.
- **Best campaign:** None earned a click or sale, so there's no true winner.
  On visibility alone, *Cat Tunnel Bed Pink Only* (45 impressions) and
  *PT Pet Odor Eliminator* (15 impressions) are getting seen the most.
- **Worst campaigns:** Five campaigns recorded **0 impressions** — they aren't
  serving at all (likely bids far too low, or very low search volume targets).
- **Wasted spend / negatives to add:** **None this period** — there was no spend,
  so there is no wasted spend and nothing to add as a negative keyword yet.
- **Search terms to add as exact-match:** **None yet.** No search term has
  produced a click or sale, so there's nothing proven to promote to exact match.
  The keyword with the most visibility is "pet odor eliminator" (13% top-of-search
  share) — worth watching once bids are raised.

### What to do next
1. **Raise bids** on the campaigns that are showing but not clicking
   (Cat Tunnel Bed, PT Pet Odor Eliminator, the "pet odor eliminator" phrase
   campaign). Current bids are too low to win clicks — try increasing them
   meaningfully (e.g. +30–50%) and re-check in the next report.
2. **Check the 0-impression campaigns** — confirm they're enabled, in budget,
   and that their bids aren't near the floor. Some targets may simply have too
   little search volume.
3. **Confirm the account is meant to be live.** Two straight periods with
   ~$0 spend and no clicks suggests either intentionally paused activity or
   bids that need a real reset. If it should be running, the priority is
   getting bids competitive so the $40/day budget can actually be used.
