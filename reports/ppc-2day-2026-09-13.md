**What's really going on (the important part):**
Your ads stopped serving on **3 September** and haven't run since. When they *were*

**made zero sales the entire time**. So there are two problems stacked on top of each
other: (1) the ads are now switched off/blocked somehow, and (2) even before that they
weren't converting.

**What to do next (in order):**
1. **Check why nothing is serving.** In Seller Central / Campaign Manager, confirm the
   campaigns aren't paused, the campaign/portfolio budgets aren't exhausted, and there's
   no billing or payment problem on the account.
2. **Check the product listing.** Zero impressions across every campaign usually points to
   the product itself — is ASIN **B0FXW3GW5F** in stock, buyable, holding the Buy Box, and
   not suppressed? Ads won't show if the listing can't be advertised.
3. **Check bids.** If everything above is fine, bids may simply be far too low to win any
   placements — review and raise bids on the main Exact and Auto campaigns.
4. **Once ads are serving again, fix conversion.** The pre-3-Sep data made no sales, so
   review the listing's price, images, title, reviews and relevance of the targeted
   keywords before scaling spend.
