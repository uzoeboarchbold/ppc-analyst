## Part B — Summary (plain English)

**The headline: the account barely ran over these two days.** Across all 15
campaigns we got about **400 ad views (impressions), just 2 clicks, and spent
$0.33**. There were **no orders and no sales**. Because nothing sold, ACOS
(ad cost as a % of sales) can't be calculated and ROAS (sales per $1 of ad
spend) is 0.

- **Total spend:** $0.33
- **Total sales:** $0.00
- **ACOS:** n/a (no sales)
- **ROAS:** 0.00
- **Orders:** 0

**Best campaign:** *"SP | Cat Tunnel Bed Pink Only | Exact Ranking"* — the only
campaign that actually attracted clicks (2 clicks from 58 views, a healthy 3.45%
click rate) at a cheap $0.17 per click. It didn't convert to a sale yet, but it's
the only one getting real engagement.

**Worst campaigns:** Effectively the whole rest of the list — most campaigns
showed almost no activity. Several got a handful of impressions but zero clicks
(e.g. the "Pet Odor Eliminator Phrase" campaign got 191 views but no clicks),
and eight campaigns got no impressions at all. None spent money, so there's no
"wasted spend" to worry about — the problem is the opposite: the ads are
hardly being shown or clicked.

**Wasted spend / suggested negatives:** None to flag. Total spend was $0.33, far
too little to identify wasteful keywords or justify adding negatives yet.

**Well-converting search terms to add as exact-match:** None yet. The only
search term with any activity was **"cat tunnel bed"** (2 clicks, phrase match),
but it hasn't produced a sale, so it's too early to promote it to exact-match.

**What to do next:**
1. **Fix delivery, not waste.** The core issue is that ads are getting almost no
   impressions or clicks. Check that campaigns are enabled, in-budget, and that
   bids aren't set too low to win placements — top-of-search impression share is
   near 0% on almost everything, which usually means bids are too low.
2. **Lean into the one that's working.** The Cat Tunnel Bed campaign is getting
   clicks cheaply; consider a modest bid/budget increase to gather enough data to
   judge conversion.
3. **Watch "cat tunnel bed."** Keep an eye on it over the next few days; if it
   starts converting, add it as an exact-match keyword.
4. **Re-check the profile setup.** The profile id in the environment isn't a
   usable Ads profile id (see note above) — worth confirming the intended
   account/marketplace so future automation is unambiguous.
