### What to do next
The problem here is not performance, it's **delivery**: your ads are turned on but
not showing. The usual causes, quickest to check first:

1. **Bids too low.** If your keyword/product-target bids are below the going rate,
   Amazon won't place your ads. Raise bids to at least the suggested range and
   watch for impressions to start.
2. **Buy Box / listing eligibility.** Sponsored Products only serve when your
   listing holds the Buy Box and is in stock. Confirm the advertised ASINs are
   in stock, buyable, and winning the Buy Box.
3. **Budget reaching the campaign.** Daily budget is set to $40, so budget isn't
   the blocker, but confirm the campaigns have active ad groups with valid
   targets and that start dates aren't in the future.
4. **Targets present.** Make sure each campaign actually has keywords/product
   targets attached and that they're enabled (not paused).

Once impressions start flowing, the next report will have real numbers to optimise.
