## Part B — Summary (plain English)

**The short version:** the account was almost completely quiet over these two
days. It showed 63 ad impressions, got **1 click**, spent **$0.98**, and made
**no sales**. That single click did not turn into a purchase, so for now the
$0.98 is unproductive spend — but it is a tiny amount and one click is far too
little to judge anything by.

- **Total spend:** $0.98
- **Total sales:** $0.00
- **ACOS:** not applicable (you can't divide by zero sales) — effectively 100% wasted so far
- **ROAS:** 0.00 (no sales returned on the spend)
- **Clicks:** 1 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Impressions:** 63 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **CTR:** 1.59% &nbsp;|&nbsp; **CPC:** $0.98

**Best campaign:** Nothing "won" because there were no sales. The only campaign
that got a click — *SP Isolated … Pet Odor Eliminator Phrase* — at least proved
it can show at the top of search (12% top-of-search share) and earn clicks
(7.7% click-through). It's the one showing early signs of life.

**Worst campaign:** Also *SP Isolated … Pet Odor Eliminator Phrase* — it is the
only campaign that spent money ($0.98) and returned nothing. But with a single
click, that's noise, not a verdict.

**Wasted spend / negatives to consider:** None worth acting on yet. All spend
came from one keyword (`pet odor eliminator`, phrase match) via the search term
**"pet odor eliminator spray"** — 1 click, no sale. That is *not* enough
evidence to add a negative; one non-converting click is normal. Revisit if this
term keeps taking clicks without ever converting.

**Well-converting search terms to add as exact match:** None this period — there
were zero converting search terms to promote.

**The bigger picture:** the real issue isn't ACOS or wasted spend, it's
**volume**. Ten of the eleven campaigns are getting almost no impressions, and
several are getting none at all. The account is barely being served to shoppers,
which usually points to bids and/or budgets being too low to enter the auction,
or very new campaigns still in Amazon's "learning" ramp-up.

**What to do next:**
1. **Raise visibility, not cut spend.** With under $1 of spend there's nothing to
   trim. The lever this period is getting impressions — nudge bids up on the
   campaigns sitting at 0–5 impressions so they actually enter auctions.
2. **Check budgets aren't capping.** Confirm the $40/day account budget (and any
   per-campaign budgets) aren't set so low that campaigns stop serving early.
3. **Give the one live keyword a little room.** `pet odor eliminator` (phrase) is
   the only term drawing traffic — let it gather ~10 clicks before judging it.
4. **Leave negatives alone for now.** No term has enough clicks to justify one.
5. This is the **baseline** — next report will show whether volume is climbing.
